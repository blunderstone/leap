## How to Get Listed

If you have made a contribution to this repository (bug fix, feature, documentation, or design improvement),
please follow our [Contribution Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) to submit a pull request.

As part of your pull request, you are encouraged to add your name, GitHub profile link, and a brief description of your
contribution directly to this `CONTRIBUTORS.md` file. See the "F. Andy Seidl" link at the top for a formatting example.

By submitting your pull request, you also ensure that you have agreed to our Contribution Licensing Terms detailed in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
